Common Questions

Accent wall FAQ

Can you install just one accent wall, without a full renovation?
Yes. A single feature wall is one of our most common standalone projects — no remodel required.
What does a custom accent wall cost in Boca Raton?
It depends on material and scale — a wood slat wall and a full stone feature wall are very different projects. We give a firm quote after seeing the space, free of charge.
Do you handle both design and installation?
Yes, in-house. The team that designs the wall builds it, so there’s no handoff gap.
How long does a typical feature wall take?
Most standalone accent walls are completed in days, not weeks. Exact timing depends on material and complexity.

Have a wall that needs a reason to exist?

Tell us the room and the look you’re after. We’ll take it from there.
