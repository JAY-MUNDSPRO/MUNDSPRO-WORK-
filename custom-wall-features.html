Common Questions

Accent wall FAQ

Can you install just one accent wall, without a full renovation? Yes — a single feature wall is one of our most common standalone projects. No full remodel required.
What does a custom accent wall cost in Boca Raton? It depends on material and scale — a wood slat wall and a full stone feature wall are very different projects. We give a firm quote after seeing the space, free of charge.
Do you handle both design and installation? Yes, in-house — the same team that designs the wall builds it, so there's no handoff gap.
How long does a typical feature wall take? Most standalone accent walls are completed in days, not weeks — exact timing depends on material and complexity.
